# Retail-Sales-Performance-and-Customer-Value-Analysis
This  project demonstrates how the integration of data analytics, business intelligence and customer segmentation can be employed to draw insights from business data to aid in business decisions by making use of a combination of SQL, Python, and Tableau.
## Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing retail sales performance and customer value to generate actionable business insights that can support data-driven decision-making. The analysis examines sales trends, product performance, profitability, customer purchasing behaviour, discount effectiveness, and customer segmentation. Using Python, SQL, and Tableau, the project transforms raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights, with particular attention to identifying revenue drivers, evaluating customer value, and understanding opportunities for improving customer retention and profitability.
## Business Problem
Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data, but without proper analysis, it can be difficult to understand what drives sales performance and customer profitability. This project addresses these challenges through an integrated sales performance and customer value analysis.
## Project Objective
The goal of this project is to analyse retail sales performance and to demonstrate how data-driven decision-making can help retail businesses strengthen customer relationships to boost the financial performance of the business entity by carrying out the following objectives:
1. Evaluate overall sales performance and profitability.
2. Identify high-performing and underperforming product categories.
3. Analyse sales trends across different periods.
4. Assess customer purchasing patterns and contribution to revenue.
5. Examine the relationship between discounts, sales, and profit.
6. Segment customers based on purchasing behaviour using RFM analysis.
7. Identify opportunities for customer retention and improved profitability.
8. Develop interactive dashboards to communicate findings.

## Business Questions
1. Which product categories generate the most revenue and profit?
2. Which months or quarters perform best?
3. Which customer segment or region contributes the most revenue and profit? And how do revenue, profit, and average order value (AOV) differ by customer segment?
4. How are discounts associated with sales, profit, and profit margin? Which discounts are effective at increasing sales without destroying profitability?
5. How can customers be segmented using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) value? And which customers can be classified as "Champions","Loyal","Potential Loyalist","At Risk","Hibernating", or "Lost"?

## Dataset Description
The dataset is a synthetic one generated by ChatGPT and made up of 60,000 rows of sales transaction data and 17 different retail sales variables of interest, comprising the following:

OrderID: Unique order identification of items
OrderDate: The date the order was made
CustomerID: Unique customer identification
CustomerName: Names of customers
CustomerSegment: Category of customer
Product: Items being purchased
Category: Category of product purchased
Region: Location of customers
Quantity: Number of items purchased
Sales: Prices of items purchased
Discount: Discount on each item
Profit: Profit made on each item
ShipMode: The type of shipment used for each item
PaymentMethod: Mode of payment used to purchase the item
SignupDate: Customer subscription date

## SQL Analysis
SQLite was used to perform structured queries and answer key business questions.
The SQL analysis focused on:
* Total sales, profit, and profit margins.
* Monthly and quarterly revenue trends.
* Product category performance.
* Regional and customer segment performance.
* Customer purchasing contribution.
* Discount and profitability analysis.
* Customer-level sales aggregation.
* RFM Segmentation to classify customers based on
  1. Recency: How recently a customer made a purchase.
  2. Frequency: How frequently a customer purchased.
  3. Monetary: How much a customer spent.
  
  Customers were classified into segments including:
Champions, Loyal Customers, Potential Loyalists, At Risk, Hibernating, and Lost.
The analysis provides a framework for identifying valuable customers, customers with retention opportunities, and inactive customer groups.

  ## Key Findings

1. The product category contributing most to total revenue and profit is the Electronic category of products.
2. The regions and customer segments with the strongest financial performance are the Northern region and Small business categories, respectively.
3. The second quarter of 2024 is the strongest in terms of revenue.
4. 20% to 30% discount levels are associated with lower profitability
5. 16% of the customers are classified as Champions, 26% as Loyal, 18% as At Risk, 11% as Potential Loyalist, 7% as Hibernating, and 22% as Lost.
6. Loyal customers and Champions together contributed over 54% of the overall revenue
7. With At Risk customers contributing over 20% of the revenue, customer retention strategies could be applied to these customers to improve performance.
   
## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, recommendations should focus on:

1. Strengthening inventory and marketing strategies around profitable products.
2. Reviewing discount policies to protect profit margins.
3. Developing targeted loyalty programmes for high-value customers.
4. Implementing re-engagement campaigns for at-risk and inactive customers.
5. Improving customer retention through personalised marketing.
6. Monitoring regional and customer segment performance regularly.
